Privacy Policy

1. Who operates the service

HERMES Sport Platform is operated by Matthieu FELIX, an individual developer located in France (the “Operator”). The Operator is responsible for the personal data processed by this private service.

Privacy contact: matthieu_felix@yahoo.fr.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to HERMES Sport Platform and its optional connection to HUAWEI Health through the official Health Service Kit cloud API. The legal-information website itself contains no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, account system, or contact form.

3. Data obtained from HUAWEI Health

HERMES Sport Platform requests access only after the user signs in to HUAWEI and expressly authorizes the requested read permissions. Depending on the workout and the permissions the user grants, the service may receive:

  • exercise-record identifiers, type, start time, end time, duration, and status;
  • running metrics such as distance, speed or pace, calories, ascent, altitude, cadence, and heart rate;
  • laps and time-series samples when supplied by HUAWEI Health;
  • workout route and location points only when location access is separately authorized;
  • limited device and provenance metadata needed to identify and synchronize the workout;
  • OAuth tokens and minimal technical identifiers needed to maintain the authorized connection.

The integration does not request permission to write, modify, or delete HUAWEI Health data. It does not request general health records, body composition, sleep, or daily activity data for this test integration.

4. Why the data is used

The imported data is used only to:

  • synchronize workouts selected by the authorized account;
  • normalize workout information into consistent units and timestamps;
  • display personal running history, summaries, trends, and performance indicators;
  • support user-requested, non-medical running analysis within the private service;
  • protect the service, diagnose synchronization failures, and prevent duplicate imports.

The data is not used for advertising, profiling for third-party marketing, sale, or data brokerage. No automated decision produces legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Authorization and user control

Processing begins only after the user requests the connection and grants permission through the HUAWEI authorization screen. The user may grant only some permissions. Refusing or withdrawing a permission prevents the related feature but does not prevent use of unrelated local features.

Authorization can be withdrawn in HUAWEI Health through the privacy or third-party-services settings for HUAWEI Health Service Kit. The user may also disconnect HUAWEI Health from HERMES Sport Platform. Disconnecting stops future synchronization and removes locally held OAuth credentials; it does not automatically erase workouts already imported.

6. Storage, disclosure, and transfers

HUAWEI Health data imported during the private test is stored on the Operator’s self-hosted, access-controlled HERMES Sport Platform system. The Huawei synchronization feature does not sell the data or disclose it to advertisers or data brokers. Imported HUAWEI Health data is not sent to an external AI or research provider as part of this test integration.

Communications with HUAWEI and access to the private service use HTTPS. Credentials are kept separately from application source code, and access to the service is restricted. HUAWEI processes account authentication and Health Service Kit requests under HUAWEI’s own privacy terms. GitHub may process ordinary web-server information when a visitor opens this public legal-information website; this website does not add its own tracking.

7. Retention and deletion

OAuth credentials are retained only while the HUAWEI Health connection remains active and are removed when the connection is disconnected locally. Imported workout records are kept until the user uses the deletion function or asks the Operator to delete them. Security and diagnostic records, when needed, are minimized and must not contain OAuth secrets, precise route points, or raw workout payloads.

Revoking HUAWEI authorization stops new collection. To erase previously imported information, the user must make a separate deletion request using the contact address above. Deletion from HERMES Sport Platform does not delete the original records held by HUAWEI Health.

8. User rights

Subject to applicable law, the user may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability of personal data held by HERMES Sport Platform. Requests can be sent to the privacy contact above. The Operator may need to verify that the requester controls the connected account before acting on a request.

9. Security and incident handling

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, including restricted access, HTTPS transport, isolated credential storage, least-privilege permissions, and secret redaction. No system is completely secure. If an incident creates a risk to the user’s data, the Operator will investigate, contain it, revoke affected credentials where appropriate, and provide any notice required by applicable law.

10. Children

The service is a private developer test and is not directed to children. It must not be connected to a child’s HUAWEI Health account.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated when the service, requested permissions, or legal requirements change. The effective date and version shown above will be updated. A material change to the HUAWEI Health data use will require a fresh review and, where required, renewed user consent.
